# Decision Record Template

Date: YYYY-MM-DD  
Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by DR-XXXX

## Context
What issue are we seeing, or what design decision are we facing right now?

Write this section for a future developer who was not in the room.
Include practical constraints (time, security, tooling, team familiarity, demo needs).

## Decision
What are we choosing, exactly?

Be specific enough that someone can implement or verify the choice.
If this decision is temporary, say what would trigger a revisit.

## Also considered
List the alternatives we discussed and why we did not choose them.

Keep this honest. Future developers should be able to understand tradeoffs, not just the final answer.
